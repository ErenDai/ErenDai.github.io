Emberline: Wake the Gates

A one-touch swinging platformer for iPhone · Developer: Eren Dai

How to play

  • Hold anywhere on the screen to cast your line to the nearest anchor and swing.
  • While holding, slide your finger up or down to climb or lower the line.
  • Let go to fly. Reach the glowing gate to finish the level.
  • Collect three embers in each level, beat the par time for a sigil and find the 12 hidden echoes.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. All progress is saved on your device.

I changed phones and lost my progress.
Progress is stored only on the device, so it does not move to a new phone. Sorry about that.

A level is too hard.
Waystones inside longer levels save your place, so you only repeat the last part. Every level can be finished.

How do I change the language?
Settings > Language (English or Turkish).

How do I change my privacy choices for ads?
In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: Settings > Privacy choices. See also the Privacy Policy.
