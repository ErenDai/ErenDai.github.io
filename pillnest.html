FAQ

I don't get reminders.
Open iPhone Settings > Notifications > PillNest and make sure notifications are allowed. Check that a Focus mode is not silencing PillNest. In the app, Settings > Send a test reminder checks that everything works.

Can I mark a dose from the notification?
Yes. Press and hold the notification and choose "Mark as taken" or "Snooze".

Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your data.

How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone?
Open Settings > Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Is PillNest medical advice?
No. PillNest is a reminder tool. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
