FAQ

How do I play? Read the color a customer wants, tap the colored bottles to pour them into the cauldron (for example Red + Yellow = Orange), then tap the cauldron to serve. The Recipe Book in the game shows every mix.

Where is my progress saved? Your coins, stars, levels, shop items and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Why do I see ads? The game is free and supported by ads. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch one for double coins, 15 extra seconds or an extra life.

How do I change the language? Open Settings (the gear button on the map) and choose English or Türkçe.
