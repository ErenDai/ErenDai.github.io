Privacy Policy

App: PillNest: Pill Reminder (iOS) · Developer: Eren Dai · Last updated: October 30, 2026

Summary

PillNest does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Data stored on your device

The medicines you add (name, strength, type, colour, schedule, notes and stock count), the people (profiles) you create, the doses you mark as taken or skipped, and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. This information is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Settings > Delete all data in the app, deletes it.

Like other app data, it may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by the developer.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications created by your iPhone. They are scheduled on the device and do not pass through any server. You can turn them off at any time in iPhone Settings > Notifications > PillNest.

Sharing a report

If you choose Share a report, the app creates a text summary and opens the iPhone share sheet. The report goes only where you send it (for example Messages, Mail or Notes).

In-app purchase

PillNest Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether Premium has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Health information

PillNest is a reminder tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. The app does not use Apple Health (HealthKit) and does not share health information with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available on this page with its date.
