Privacy Policy

App: Emberline: Wake the Gates (iOS) · Developer: Eren Dai · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Summary

The game does not require an account and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. Game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads provided by Unity Ads, which may collect some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, embers, sigils, echoes, sparks, ribbon colours, cloaks and settings (such as language, sound and graphics quality) are saved locally on your device only. This data is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Update check

When the game starts, it reads a small public file from this website to see whether a newer version must be installed. Only the request itself is made (as with opening any web page); nothing about you or your game is sent.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game uses the Unity Ads SDK to show full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads. The game does not ask for permission to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity may collect and process information such as:

  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device identifiers, such as the identifier for vendors,
  • ad interaction data (for example, ads shown, watched and tapped), and diagnostic and performance data.

How Unity uses this information is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can open Settings > Privacy choices in the game to change how your data is used for advertising.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available on this page with its date.
