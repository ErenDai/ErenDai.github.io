Privacy Policy

App: Don't Touch Red: Dodge (iOS) · Developer: Eren Dai · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Summary

The game does not require an account and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. Game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), which may collect some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, high scores, level progress, unlocked items and settings (such as sound and music) are saved locally on your device only. This data is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game uses the Unity Ads SDK (Unity Technologies) to show full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device identifiers, such as the identifier for vendors and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA),
  • ad interaction data (for example, ads shown and taps), and diagnostic data.

How Unity uses this information is described here: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent when it first starts, before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later in the game under Settings > Privacy choices. You can also limit ad tracking in your iPhone settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available on this page with its date.
