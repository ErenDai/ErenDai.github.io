FAQ

Where is my progress saved?
Your coins, scores, levels and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Why do I see ads?
The game is free and supported by ads. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch one to continue a run or to receive bonus coins.
